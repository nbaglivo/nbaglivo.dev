The AI engineer manifesto

Aug 11, 2026

  • You'll decide what's yours to own before the AI is in the room.
  • You'll make every decision that tracks to something that matters.
  • You'll have an opinion about the things that matter. You won't let the AI form an opinion for you.
  • You won't allow situations where the AI can choice-architect you into something.
  • You won't mistake confidence for truth. You'll always ask for evidence.
  • You will feel the friction. You won't gloss it over, and you won't let plausibility pass for truth.
