---
title: "It could be simpler"
date: "Oct 18, 2026"
---

I was looking at a PR conversation going on between two people, lots of rounds, these people were honestly relentless... except it wasn't people, it was two agents, using the people's GitHub accounts, having a conversation. The owners of those accounts presumably knew what was going on and even had a certain level of participation in letting this conversation evolve, they also noticed it in their token usage bills, "My agent is spending way too many tokens reviewing this other dude's agent work" they said.

I thought it was hilarious, demented even. Why are we having two agents discussing changes on GitHub? This could be way simpler. 

And that's something I keep coming back to in every situation of my life lately, not only in software, I keep saying to myself "this could be simpler", it feels like we are artificially adding complexity to everything in life.

This threw me back to think of the Local First community, much of their thinking comes from the fact that all this huge infrastructure that we have built where my computer in Spain talks to a server in San Francisco adds huge complexity that in many cases is not needed, it could, you guessed it, be simpler.

I have also been thinking of Software Architecture a lot, at work we have lots of Agents making a lot of code, and a lot of decisions. We are concerned with how this will play out in the future, if this will just hit a wall at some point where we can't add more stuff, where all architecture and boundaries just disappeared. I have been coming up with ideas to mitigate drifting, so creating a system for change to happen in the Agentic world, one that is not wild wild west, it's fun work, but then it struck me... what if we did not have to worry about architecture... and no, don't kill me, I'm not saying let's just let the Agents do their thing, it'll be fine. I'm just thinking, what if we need architecture because we have complexity, but, is this complexity real? or have we just manufactured it? The systems that we are building are complex, but I'm starting to think that's just our mindset, we operate in the cloud, we have multi-tenant services, tons of data; that's a lot of complexity to start with... but what if we just didn't build complex systems anymore? What if we just vibe coded silly projects, simple projects, that just need to work for the user who will use it, in their local computer even? You can vibe that without thinking of Architecture... I know, that's not how software works, we need collaboration, we need to share, things run on the internet... That's fine, some infrastructure has to stay, the sync engines, the crdts, that stuff that the Local First people are building will have a place, and it will be complex, real engineering will have a place there, but we might just need a few complex things so the rest can be simple. I don't know, it feels like it is worth experimenting with this.
