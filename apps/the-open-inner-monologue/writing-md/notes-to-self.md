Things I'd like to make sure I don't forget.   


1. Frustration is natural, endure **discomfort**, it is inevitable and **manageable**.
2. Everything has a trade-off. Understand the risks you're choosing.
3. We move forward by doing. Avoid overthinking. Use intuition. Act small.
4. Don't take things so seriously.
5. Feel **connection** and **excitement** with your work — **chase that feeling**.
6. When the body wanders, the mind follows — **walks invite creativity**.
7. Motivation appears while **doing**, not before.
8. Step away — **time and distance often finish the thought**.
9. Craft with **care and authenticity**.
